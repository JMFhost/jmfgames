🎪 Ragdoll Mayhem Arena Guide

Inspired by Kick the Buddy: Forever, this physics sandbox lets you pick from a lineup of 6 quirky ragdoll characters and test out slapstick props, sci-fi gadgets, and elemental hazards.

  • Touch & Drag: Use Gravity Grab to grab any limb and fling the character off the walls, or tap anywhere in the arena to fire/spawn your selected prop.
  • Character Lineup: Switch anytime between Sir Reginald, CLANK-9000, Zorp the Alien, Lord Bonesworth, Gloop the Slime, and Capt. Cardboard.
  • Refresh & Minimize Proof: Every coin, hit, unlock, and ragdoll state is saved automatically. When you minimize or close the browser with Auto-Bot ON, your drones keep racking up hits and coins in the background!
