About JMFgames

JMF Games Pocket Console is a free pocket-console game system. Pick a game on the screen and play it with the D-pad and buttons. Every game is free to play in your web browser — no signup, no download, and no real money involved.

The main menu opens on Retro Games, a collection of nineteen quick games — listed in alphabetical order below.

Every game opens with the animated JMF Games production splash — "A JMF Games Pocket Console Production" / "Developed and owned by JMF Games" — the console credit screen that brands every game. Press A or B to skip it.

  • 8 Ball Pool — real 8-ball pool on a real table with real numbered balls and real physics: friction, spin/english, cushion bounces, and momentum transfer. Pick one of five AI opponents — Shark (Precision), Bruiser (Power), Ghost (Tactics), Trickster (Angles), or Ace (All-round) — each with a genuinely different playing style: Shark takes pots Ghost passes up for safeties, Ghost hides the cue ball, Trickster plays real bank shots with spin. Sink your group (solids or stripes, decided by the first ball pocketed), then the 8-ball, to win. Master seven trick-shot challenges, build a win streak, and rematch your rival. Left/Right aims, Up/Down sets power, A shoots, B fine-aims (and sets spin).
  • Blackjack Royale — classic blackjack against the dealer, now with two AI table-mates (ROSA and MAX) who play real basic strategy — sharp at the High Roller table, sloppy at the low table — so the table feels alive. Cards now deal like a real table: they fly in from the shoe one at a time, and the dealer's hole card flips over when it's revealed. Start with $1,000 and be the first to $10,000. Win-streak bonuses, a High Roller table that unlocks at $2,500, and persistent achievements. Dealer stands on all 17; 3:2 blackjack payout; split pairs.
  • BLOCK DROP — a falling-block stacking game. Seven piece shapes drop from the top of a 10-wide well — move, rotate, and drop them to fill rows and clear them. Clear one line for 100 points, two for 300, three for 500, and all four at once for an 800-point QUAD. Every 10 lines the pieces fall faster, and a next-piece preview helps you plan ahead. The pieces are dealt fairly (a shuffled bag of all seven, then reshuffled), so no piece is ever starved. Left/Right moves, Up or A rotates, Down soft-drops, B hard-drops the piece to the bottom, Start pauses. Best score saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Block Quest — a block puzzle on a bigger 9×9 board. Fill rows and columns to clear them and score — now with combo chains that multiply your bonus, a Hold slot (B swaps pieces), levels every 6 lines cleared, and a +200 Perfect Clear bonus for emptying the whole board. The piece generator adapts to how you're playing: bigger pieces when you're on a roll, smaller ones when you're struggling. A fresh tray always contains a fitting piece when one exists.
  • CARD QUEST — a deckbuilding roguelike. Pick one of six original heroes — BRYN, SABLE, ORIN, LYRA, FENN, or VESK — and build a deck from 300+ unique cards as you fight through 3 acts and a final boss. Earn points to unlock new heroes, find 40 relics and 20 random events along the way, and upgrade your cards at rest sites and shops. Every run is different: new map, new rewards, new deck.
  • Chess Club — chess against the computer, with five difficulty levels that genuinely play at their strength (Easy 800 to Grandmaster 2400) — real evaluation, tactics (forks, pins, skewers), and endgame play. Now with tactics puzzles (including a daily puzzle), a player Elo rating that moves with your results, win streaks, and a hint (B, −10 rating). Full rules: castling, en passant, check, checkmate, stalemate, promotion.
  • Emberfall Brawl — a roguelite auto-battler. Pick one of five heroes — BRAM, KIRA, ASH, NOVA (Ember Mage) and ORIN (Stone Guardian) — and your hero fights on his own with smart AI. You make the calls: each hero now has FIVE ultimates — the D-pad fires four of them and B unleashes the signature fifth, each on its own cooldown — plus A summons allies to help for 5 seconds (20-second cooldown). Ultimates are manual only, they never fire on their own. NOVA's signature summons an Ember Beast that fights beside her for 5 seconds. Every wave ends with a boss — EMBER LORD, CINDER MAW and ASH SPITTER rotate through, each with its own telegraphed attacks — and the wave isn't clear until the boss falls. Between waves you choose upgrades. Survive as many waves as you can — one death ends the run. Run stats and per-hero records are tracked.
  • Haven's Stand — a zombie-apocalypse shelter manager. Build your house floor by floor going up, keep your survivors fed, watered and healthy, and gather Food, Water, Scrap, Medicine and Ammo across sixteen room types — mess hall, garden beds, rain catcher, med bay, watch post, armory and more. When night falls, zombie waves attack the gate and raider gangs try to loot your storeroom, so post guards, build traps and fight back. Send survivors out on timed scavenging expeditions, deal with wandering traders and strangers at the gate, and survive as many days as you can — when a survivor dies, they're gone for good. Three save slots, and you can delete any save. D-pad navigates, A builds and confirms, B speeds up time, Start pauses.
  • Overlord's Bastion — a lane siege battle. Command your troops against the Saint and destroy their castle. Four mirrored troop tiers on each side — Beast against Knight, Gorgon against Mage, Demon against Champion, Devil against Hero — plus two magic spells per side: you cast LIGHTNING (a single-target bolt that one-shots a Knight) and FIREBALL (a blast that burns a whole lane), while the Saint answers with PLAGUE (damage over time) and CURSE (weakens a lane's attacks). The Saint stays aggressive (same 8/s mana as you, never toned down) but now plays smart: it counters your troops by lane, times its spells for clumps and big pushes, manages its mana, and adapts to your strategy — and it gets smarter with every Saint in the 5-Saint campaign, Apprentice to Grand Saint. Take on the campaign or a Quick Battle. The troops are drawn as detailed pixel figures with walk cycles, attack animations, and death falls, and the castles show their damage as their HP falls.
  • PINBALL — an original deep-sea reef pinball table. Launch the ball with the plunger, keep it alive with two flippers, and rack up points on three jellyfish pop bumpers, starfish targets, PEARL rollover lanes, and the WAVE RAMP. Light all three rollover lanes for the REEF BONUS, knock down all three starfish for PEARL STORM multiball (three balls, double scoring), and chain ramp shots for combo multipliers. B nudges the table to save a draining ball — nudge too much and it TILTS. Particle bursts, ball trails, screen shake, flashing lights, and floating score popups on every big hit. Three balls per game; best score saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • RAGDOLL RUCKUS — Fling STITCH the burlap ragdoll around a toy-room arena with 9 unlockable weapons, from Fists to a Bomb — plus the new WHIP and ANVIL. STITCH now fights back smart: he dodges telegraphed swings, sidesteps projectiles, and counters when you overcommit. Real ragdoll physics: jointed limbs, momentum, bounce, and impact-scaled spin. Chain hits into combos to earn coins, complete four challenges, and unlock bigger toys. D-pad aims, A hits (or buys), B cycles weapons, Start pauses.
  • Sky Soar — fly through the pipes and see how far you can go. Now with real flight physics (gravity, flap momentum, terminal velocity, wind gusts), a PERFECT gap bonus, ramping pipe speed, and five unlockable birds — EMBER at 10, AZURE at 25, GOLDEN at 50, SHADOW at 100. A flaps, B dives.
  • Slingshot Siege — an original slingshot siege game with real projectile physics: gravity, a visible wind that changes per stage and bends your shot, bounce, and different projectile weights. Aim the slingshot, set your power, and launch stones, boulders, and bombs — plus new BOUNCE (ricochets) and SPLIT (bursts into three) projectiles — at enemy forts across one hundred challenge stages. Every 10th stage is a boss stage with a 3-HP armoured boss on its own keep. Destroy every target to clear the stage and unlock the next. Left/Right aims, Up/Down sets power, A launches, B cycles projectiles. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Solitaire — classic Klondike solitaire with real cards. Build the tableau down in alternating colors and stack the foundations up by suit from Ace to King. Now with scoring (foundation +10, uncovering cards +5, win bonus), win streaks, a daily deal (the same shuffle for everyone each day), a live timer, and a smart hint (B) that ranks every legal move and explains why — uncovering a face-down card beats a safe foundation move, and it warns you when a foundation move is risky. D-pad moves the cursor, A picks up and drops cards, B cancels (or hints).
  • STAR STRIKE — Blast waves of invaders in this vertical space shooter: drones, fighters, tanks and kamikazes, with a boss flagship every 5 waves. Enemies now fly in formations, dodge your shots, and bosses have three phases with new attack patterns each. Between waves, spend your score in the Upgrade Shop (Engine, Rapid Fire, Armor, Bombs, Lives). Your ship has real inertia — thrust accelerates, drag slows it, no instant stops. Grab power-ups for triple shot, shields, bombs and extra lives. D-pad flies, A fires (hold to auto-fire), B drops a bomb, Start pauses.
  • Subway Runner — an endless runner down the subway tracks with real jump and slide physics: a true jump arc, smooth lane glides (no teleporting), and slide momentum. Dodge trains, jump barriers and gaps, slide under overhead bars, and grab coins across twelve challenge stages — each with its own goal to clear to unlock the next — plus a daily stage. New power-ups (MAGNET, SHIELD, 2X COINS) and moving barriers. Every row is guaranteed passable, and the difficulty adapts to how you're playing. Left/Right switches lanes, Up jumps, Down slides. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Texas Hold'em — 5-handed poker against four named AI opponents (VIC, LUNA, DOC, and COLE), each with a genuinely different playing style — VIC is aggressive and bluffs often, LUNA is tight and only plays strong hands, DOC is balanced and adapts to how you play, COLE calls wide and almost never folds. Start with $1,000 and be the first to $10,000 — or win a tournament as the blinds climb every 5 hands. Win-streak bonuses and achievements.

Build your own park: 18 rides, food and gift stalls, restrooms, First Aid, trash cans, and decorations, plus cleaners, engineers, entertainers, and security to hire.   • VOLLEY SQUAD — turn-based artillery, and you're outnumbered: you and two AI teammates against four AI enemies. First you see the enemy squad, then you pick your map from four — open field, twin peaks, canyon, or high ground, each with its own layout, cover, and advantages — then you pick one of sixteen original vehicles and your two AI teammates from a roster of sixteen pilots, each with their own personality and vehicle. TITAN the heavy siege tank, WASP the triple-shot buggy, LANCE the rail sniper, RICOCHET the bouncy bomber, and twelve more, each with its own weapon, stats, passive ability, and ultimate. Left/Right aims, Up/Down sets power, A fires, B unleashes your ultimate, Start pauses. Wins and best damage save automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.

On a keyboard, the arrow keys (or WASD) work as the D-pad, X is the A button, Z is the B button, Enter is Start, and Shift is Menu. The Menu button takes you back to the list you launched the game from, and B steps back a level in the menus. Every game has background music, and the SOUND and MUSIC buttons below the Menu and Start buttons on the console turn the sound effects and the music on or off.
