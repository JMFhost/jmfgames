About JMFgames

JMFgames is a free pocket-console game system. When you open jmfgames.com, you'll see the JMF Games console — pick a game on the screen and play it with the D-pad and buttons. Every game is free to play in your web browser — no signup, no download, and no real money involved.

The console currently has fifteen games, listed in alphabetical order on the menu:

  • 8 Ball Pool — real 8-ball pool on a real table with real numbered balls. Pick one of five AI opponents — Shark, Bruiser, Ghost, Trickster, or Ace — and sink your group (solids or stripes, decided by the first ball pocketed), then the 8-ball, to win. Left/Right aims, Up/Down sets power, A shoots, B fine-aims.
  • Blackjack Royale — classic blackjack against the dealer. Start with $1,000 and be the first to $10,000.
  • BlockQuest — a block puzzle. Fill rows and columns on a 9×9 board to clear them and score.
  • Chess Club — chess against the computer, with five difficulty levels (Easy 800 to Grandmaster 2400) and a choice of White, Black, or Random.
  • Emberfall Brawl — a roguelite auto-battler. Pick one of three heroes — BRAM, KIRA, or ASH — and your hero fights on his own with smart AI. You make the calls: A summons the other two heroes to help for 3 seconds (20-second cooldown), B unleashes your hero's ultimate (10-second cooldown), and between waves you choose upgrades to make your hero stronger. Survive as many waves as you can — one death ends the run.
  • Harvest Haven — an original farming simulation. Inherit a run-down farm in the town of Maplewood and rebuild it: till soil, plant seasonal crops, water them daily, and harvest to earn gold. Fish in the pond, mine the hills for ore, forage the forest, and befriend over two hundred townspeople — nine of whom you can marry. Every townsperson has a job — blacksmith, fisher, cook, teacher, doctor, merchant, and more — and they live full lives: they farm their own plots, fish, mine, take bulletin-board requests, befriend and marry each other, and raise children. Compete with the rival farmer at the Fall Harvest Festival crop contest and climb the Town Rankings. Four seasonal festivals, animals to raise (chickens, cows, pigs, sheep, goats, and ducks), a pet dog or cat, artisan machines, a kitchen, tool upgrades, sprinklers, fertilizer and crop quality, fruit trees, beehives, a greenhouse, a beach with ocean fishing, a deeper mine with monsters to fight, weather, a museum, Town Hall restoration, a tropical island by boat, skill levels, and a story quest to restore Maplewood. D-pad walks, A uses the selected tool or talks, B cycles tools, Start pauses. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Overlord's Bastion — a lane siege battle. Command your troops against the Saint and destroy their castle. Four mirrored troop tiers on each side — Beast against Knight, Gorgon against Mage, Demon against Champion, Devil against Hero — and both castles defend every lane, so battles last much longer.
  • RAGDOLL RUCKUS — Fling STITCH the burlap ragdoll around a toy-room arena with 7 unlockable weapons, from Fists to a Bomb. Chain hits into combos to earn coins and unlock bigger toys. D-pad aims, A hits (or buys), B cycles weapons, Start pauses.
  • Sky Soar — fly through the pipes and see how far you can go.
  • Slingshot Siege — an original slingshot siege game. Aim the slingshot, set your power, and launch stones, boulders, and bombs at enemy forts across one hundred challenge stages — destroy every target to clear the stage and unlock the next. Left/Right aims, Up/Down sets power, A launches, B cycles projectiles. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Solitaire — classic Klondike solitaire with real cards. Build the tableau down in alternating colors and stack the foundations up by suit from Ace to King. D-pad moves the cursor, A picks up and drops cards, B cancels.
  • STAR STRIKE — Blast waves of invaders in this vertical space shooter: drones, fighters, tanks and kamikazes, with a boss flagship every 5 waves. Grab power-ups for triple shot, shields, bombs and extra lives. D-pad flies, A fires (hold to auto-fire), B drops a bomb, Start pauses.
  • Subway Runner — an endless runner down the subway tracks. Dodge trains, jump barriers, slide under overhead bars, and grab coins across twelve challenge stages — each with its own goal to clear to unlock the next. Left/Right switches lanes, Up jumps, Down slides. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.
  • Texas Hold'em — heads-up poker against the computer. Start with $1,000 and be the first to $10,000.
  • Thrill Park — a theme park manager. Build your own park on a massive grid: 18 rides, food and gift stalls, restrooms, First Aid, trash cans, and decorations, plus cleaners, engineers, entertainers, and security to hire. Guests walk in, queue, ride, and spend — keep them happy, keep the park clean, and work through ten goals to become a Park Legend. D-pad moves the cursor, A places, B cycles what to build, Start pauses. Progress saves automatically, and you can delete your save to start over.

On a keyboard, the arrow keys (or WASD) work as the D-pad, X is the A button, Z is the B button, Enter is Start, and Shift is Menu. The Menu button takes you back to the main menu from anywhere, and the B button does something different in each game. Every game has background music, and the SOUND and MUSIC buttons below the Menu and Start buttons on the console turn the sound effects and the music on or off.

JMFgames is built and run by JMFhost. New free games are added regularly.
