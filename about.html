About JMFgames

JMFgames is a free phone-style game system. Open the site and you get a phone: swipe up to unlock it, then tap a game app on the home screen and play. Every game is touch-first and free to play in your web browser — no signup, no download, and no real money involved.

Block Zen — our first game. A quick block puzzle you can play over and over: drag pieces onto a 9×9 board, fill full rows, columns, or 3×3 boxes to clear them, and chain combos and streaks to multiply your score. No time limit, no lives — the game ends only when no piece fits, and one tap starts a new round instantly. Your best score saves automatically, and your current game is saved after every move, so you can leave and pick up exactly where you stopped. Seven realistic photo backgrounds, relaxing sounds and music, and fair random pieces — never rigged.

Solitaire — the classic card game, rebuilt for touch. Tap a card to auto-move it, double-tap to send it to the foundations, or drag whole stacks with your finger. Every deal is fair and random, the daily deal gives everyone the same shuffle, undo is unlimited, and hints explain themselves. Your stats and best score save automatically, and your current game is saved after every move.

Blockfall — a fast falling-blocks puzzle. Pieces drop one at a time: drag to slide them, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop them faster, and fill complete lines to clear them. Clear four lines at once for the biggest bonus, hold a piece for later, and watch the ghost piece show exactly where each piece will land. The game speeds up as you level up, and your best score and stats save automatically — your current game is saved too, so you can leave and pick up exactly where you stopped.

More games are on the way, including bigger adventures. Every game auto-saves, and every game can have its save deleted to start over.

On a phone, play with your finger — drag pieces and cards, tap icons, swipe to unlock. On a computer, the mouse works the same way. Sound effects and music can be turned on or off in the phone's Settings app, and your choices are remembered.

JMFgames is built and run by JMFhost. New free games are added regularly.
